How we run the counter

Nothing fancy, just a system that works — and enough honesty to tell you exactly what you're getting.

Under three minutes, most nights

Short menu, one grill, no surprises. Order at the counter and it's usually in your hands before you've found a seat.

Fair prices, no upcharges

Extra pickles are free. Portions are generous. We're not trying to nickel-and-dime you for a late-night burger.

A short, classic menu

Three burgers, two fries, three shakes. Everything on it earns its place — nothing's there to pad the board.

Sink instead of a bathroom

We'll say it plainly — there's a wash-up sink, not a full restroom. Efficient, honest, and it keeps the line moving.
